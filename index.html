HOW TO FLY JOE!

Control Joe using your upper-body webcam tracking or simple keyboard controls!

1. Flap to Climb

Flap your arms up & down to climb altitude smoothly!

2. Tilt Arms to Turn

Tilt arms like wings to turn left or right. Level arms fly straight!

3. Hands Together Dive

Bring both hands together in front of your chest to dive downward!
